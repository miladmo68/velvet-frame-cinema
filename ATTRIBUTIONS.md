# Artwork, content and research

The interface, brand name, six fictional film titles, descriptions, schedule, data model and poster artwork were authored for this project. Posters are implemented with original CSS shapes, gradients and grain. No third-party photograph, film still, poster, font, icon library or product code was copied or bundled.

Functional research (consulted 2026-11-10):

- Cineplex official programme and showtime browsing: https://www.cineplex.com/
- TIFF Lightbox published cinema seating map: https://assets.ctfassets.net/22n7d68fswlw/1ceBmJ4HOnDn43RS5sEVPl/7eed0b2e0f657f77036ce19dcd63d16c/TIFF50_Lightbox_Maps_Cinema2.pdf
- NextTicket open-source feature reference: https://github.com/ram-sankar/NextTicket

These sources informed feature expectations only. No code, assets, film titles, business claims or protected branding were reused. All interface records are clearly illustrative.
