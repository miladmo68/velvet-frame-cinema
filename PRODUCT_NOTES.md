# Velvet Frame · product and rotation notes

## Position in the portfolio

Following Parcel & Pine (buyer-facing spatial home discovery, MERN) and Mallow & Ember (restaurant menu/cart/table planning, Next.js), Velvet Frame is a customer-facing independent cinema programme and seat-selection planner. It replaces cards/maps and table capacity with a film editorial, showtime timeline, auditorium geometry, seat inventory and ticket mix. The visual direction uses deep plum, bone and persimmon with cinematic typography and original CSS artwork.

## Research (2026-11-10)

- Cineplex's official site presents film and showtime browsing with seat previews. https://www.cineplex.com/
- TIFF Lightbox publishes seating plans that distinguish auditorium geometry and accessible positions. https://assets.ctfassets.net/22n7d68fswlw/1ceBmJ4HOnDn43RS5sEVPl/7eed0b2e0f657f77036ce19dcd63d16c/TIFF50_Lightbox_Maps_Cinema2.pdf
- Open-source NextTicket explores film/theatre selection and timed confirmation. It is a functional reference only; no source, branding or assets are copied. https://github.com/ram-sankar/NextTicket

All film titles, synopsis, schedules, availability and ticket prices here are original illustrative demo content. The app takes no payment or real reservation. No third-party artwork or fonts are bundled.
